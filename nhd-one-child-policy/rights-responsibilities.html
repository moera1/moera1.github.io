Rights

While some people agreed with the policy, most of them were not happy with the limiting of their rights. Parents were forced to choose between aborting children, hiding extra children from the government, or abandoning them.
"Everyone has the right: To choose whether to have children or not. To decide freely the number, spacing and timing of children" — World Health Organization, 2022
"Officials would kidnap you if you tried to have two children."​​​​​​​ — Lu Bilun, 2021
“Giving birth is a fundamental human right. In China, the government controls it.​​​​​​​"​​​​​​​ — Harry Wu, Laogai Research Foundation, 2004

Responsibilities

Government leaders had the responsibility to keep the country’s economy healthy. To do that, they limited the resources needed to support an increasing new generation by limiting the generation itself. They would justify taking away the reproductive rights of the people in order to achieve a growing economy.
